Light as a feather.

Your apps and what you were watching. No ads, no sponsored rows, no account. Nothing else.

Measured on a Hisense Google TV (Android 12). The stock Google TV launcher is ~25 MB and uses ~93 MB of RAM.

Everything you use. Nothing you don't.

Tiny and fast

No third-party libraries and no extra background services. Home is there the moment you press it.

No ads, no tracking

No sponsored rows, no account, no analytics. Your settings stay on your TV.

Continue Watching

Resume shows and films from your apps, including Stremio, right from the home screen.

A clean apps row

Monochrome or colour logos. Reorder, sort by most used, hide apps, or lock the layout with a PIN.

Your Home button

The Home button on your remote opens Plume, even on Google TV. Hold it for the Google TV dashboard.

Your look

Frosted or clear glass over your wallpaper. Photos, video scenes, light, dark or automatic with the sun.

Install Plume Custom

Sign in to GitHub with access to the fork and download Plume-Custom.apk from its releases.

Install with a USB drive or adb

USB drive. Copy the APK to a USB drive and open it with a file manager on the TV.

adb. Run this from a computer on the same network.

adb install -r Plume-Custom.apk

Android 13 and newer. Android greys out the Home button switch for apps installed outside the Play Store. The README explains the one-time fix.

If your build has an update feed, check for new versions in Plume settings → Updates.
